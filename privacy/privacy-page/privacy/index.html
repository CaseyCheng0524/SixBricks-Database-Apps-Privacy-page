私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

六色積木遊戲資料庫（「本應用程式」）由積夢 (香港) 有限公司（「我們」）與 Care for Education 合作開發。我們重視你的私隱，本政策說明本應用程式如何處理資料。

1. 本應用程式的使用對象

本應用程式供帶領六色積木活動的成年人使用，例如老師、治療師及培訓導師。本應用程式並非為兒童自行使用而設計，亦不會收集任何兒童的個人資料。

2. 只存放在你裝置上的資料

你的收藏、課堂計劃、已玩記錄、評分、筆記及設定，只會存放在你的裝置上。我們無法查閱這些資料。如你的裝置已開啟 iCloud 或 Google 備份，這些資料會按你的裝置設定一併備份。刪除本應用程式即會刪除這些資料。

3. 你主動提交的資料

當你使用「建議一個遊戲」功能並按下「提交建議」，我們會收到：

  • 你填寫的活動內容（名稱、分類、步驟、變化、提問等）
  • 你自願提供的姓名、學校／機構及電郵（全部選填）

這些資料只會用於：

  • 由積夢 (香港) 有限公司及 Care for Education 審閱你的建議
  • 如建議獲採納並收錄於本應用程式，按你提供的姓名作鳴謝
  • 如有需要，就你的建議與你聯絡

當你使用「意見／建議」功能並按下「提交意見」，我們會收到你的評分及選項、你的意見，以及你自願提供的姓名及電郵（選填）。這些資料只會用於改進本應用程式，以及如有需要時回覆你。

提交建議前，你必須同意有關收錄及鳴謝的條款。資料經加密連線傳送，儲存於由 Google 提供的試算表服務。

4. 我們不會收集的資料

本應用程式不需要登入，不收集你的位置、相片、聯絡人或裝置識別碼，不含廣告，亦不使用第三方分析或追蹤工具。

5. 資料保存及刪除

投稿資料會保存至審閱完成及（如獲採納）活動收錄期間。如你希望查閱、更正或刪除你提交的資料，請電郵至 info@letsbuildhk.com，我們會於 40 日內處理。

6. 政策更新

如本政策有更新，我們會更新本頁的生效日期，並在本應用程式內作出通知。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

The Six Bricks Game Database ("the App") is developed by Let’s Build (Hong Kong) ("we") in partnership with Care for Education. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Who the App is for

The App is for adults who run Six Bricks activities, such as teachers, therapists and trainers. It is not designed for children to use on their own, and it does not collect any personal information about children.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your favourites, lesson plans, played history, ratings, notes and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. If your device uses iCloud or Google backup, this information is backed up according to your device settings. Deleting the App deletes this information.

3. Information you choose to send us

When you use "Suggest an activity" and tap "Submit suggestion", we receive:

  • the activity you wrote (name, category, steps, variations, questions)
  • your name, school or organisation, and email, if you choose to provide them (all optional)

We use this only to:

  • let Let’s Build (Hong Kong) and Care for Education review your suggestion
  • credit you by the name you provided, if your activity is added to the App
  • contact you about your suggestion, if needed

When you use "Feedback" and tap "Send feedback", we receive your ratings and answers, your comments, and your name and email if you choose to provide them (optional). We use this only to improve the App and, if needed, to reply to you.

Before submitting an activity, you must agree to the terms for inclusion and credit. Information is sent over an encrypted connection and stored in a spreadsheet service provided by Google.

4. What we do not collect

The App has no accounts or sign-in. It does not collect your location, photos, contacts or device identifiers, contains no advertising, and uses no third-party analytics or tracking.

5. Keeping and deleting information

Submissions are kept while they are reviewed and, if accepted, for as long as the activity appears in the App. To access, correct or delete what you submitted, email info@letsbuildhk.com and we will respond within 40 days.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page and let you know in the App.
